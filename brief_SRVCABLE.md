# Forensic Equity Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE)
**As of:** 23 September 2026 | **Primary Exchange:** NSE | **Analyst Horizon:** Long-Term Retail

---

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) is an Indian manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors, primarily serving power utilities and the growing data-centre sector [3]. For Q1 FY27 (ended 30 June 2026), the company reported revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY), an EBITDA margin of 11.2% (down 170 bps YoY), and a Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹82 crore (+5.9% YoY) [3]. The latest available share price is ₹1,012 [1].

---

## 2. Bull Case
*   **Robust Order Book & Capacity Expansion:** The company's order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, a significant increase from ₹3,150 crore a year ago, indicating strong demand visibility [3]. Furthermore, a new plant in Bharuch, expected to add 30% to cable capacity, is on track to commence commercial production in Q3 FY27, supporting future growth [3, 2].
*   **High-Growth End-Market Exposure:** Demand from power transmission and data-centre projects remains strong [3]. Data-centre cables currently contribute 9% to domestic revenue and are projected to double in two years, tapping into a high-growth segment [2].
*   **Increasing Export Contribution:** Exports contributed 21% of total revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in Q1 FY26, demonstrating expanding international market penetration and diversification of revenue streams [3].
*   **Improved Governance & Reduced Pledging:** Promoter pledging has significantly decreased from 35% in March 2024 to a much healthier 4.1% of promoter holding as of June 2026, reducing a key governance concern for minority shareholders [4, 8].

---

## 3. Bear Case
*   **Raw Material Price Sensitivity & Margin Lag:** Operating margins contracted by 170 basis points in Q1 FY27 due to a sharp rise in copper prices, the company's primary raw material [3, 1]. While two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, there is a 1-2 quarter lag in passing on these costs, which could continue to depress interim margins [2].
*   **Elongated Receivable Days:** Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, primarily due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [2]. This ties up cash in working capital, potentially impacting cash flow generation and requiring closer monitoring.
*   **Material Contingent Liability:** The company has received a GST demand order for ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [7]. While management claims no material impact and intends to appeal, this amount represents a significant 56.5% of Q1 FY27's Profit After Tax (₹82 crore), making it a material financial risk that warrants close attention [3, 7].

However, the official company press release for the same period stated revenue as ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY) [3]. The official company filing is considered the authoritative source, indicating a likely transposition or reporting error in the news article.
*   **Governance & Materiality Inquiries:**
    *   What specific measures are being taken to address the elongated receivable days from state utility customers, and what is the expected timeline for improvement?
    *   What is the detailed breakdown of the ₹46.3 crore GST demand, and what is the estimated probability and timeline for a favorable resolution of the appeal?
    *   What is the company's strategy to mitigate the impact of raw material price volatility during the 1-2 quarter lag period before cost pass-through?
